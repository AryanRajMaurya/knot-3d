A form under tension

A torus knot, cast in glass. Scroll to approach.

Glass, not plastic

Transmission, thickness and a little iridescence.

Light has to get in

Inside the knot the material stops being a surface.

Every surface is a mirror

Angle of incidence, angle of reflection.

Made in the browser

Three.js, WebGL2, no textures. Drag to turn it.
